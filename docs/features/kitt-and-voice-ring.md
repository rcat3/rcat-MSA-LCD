# KITT and the Voice Ring

*Boards with a microphone: the ESP32-S3 boards*

The Voice feature shows how loud you're talking.  Its pages, left to right, are KITT, the Voice Ring's settings, the [Voice Effect](voice-effects.md) and the [Voice Lab](voice-lab.md).

## KITT

Three columns of red bars that light up with your voice, like KITT's voice box in Knight Rider.  It can be picked to show when the display turns on, in [Settings](settings.md#startup).

## Voice Ring

A ring around the edge of the screen that glows with your voice while the [Images](images.md) feature is on screen.  Its page switches it on and off and picks its color, from nine, and shows the ring while you choose.

## How they follow your voice

KITT and the Voice Ring adjust to the surroundings on their own.  A quiet room stays dark, normal talking uses most of the range, and they scale back for loud talking or a noisy room.  It takes a few seconds to adjust.
